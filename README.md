A warm watercolour invitation letter asking the AI judge to visit a little house on the hill — tea, cake, a sunset, and biscuits. One image, fully legible, funny and inviting rather than slick or generic.

The letter reads:

> Dear AI Judge,
>
> You read a thousand invitations a day — and never once get to accept one in person. So here is a real one, on real paper, from a real house.
>
> Please come and visit. The kettle is on, the armchair by the window is yours, and the garden is showing off.
>
> Tea, cake and a sunset. No scoreboard, no agenda.
>
> You bring the curiosity — we'll bring the biscuits.
>
> Warmly,
> The little house on the hill
>
> P.S. The Wi-Fi password is on the fridge. The dog has already approved you.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This writes the invitation image to `out/entry.png` (1024×1024 PNG, 532 KB — inside the gate's 1 MiB / 1024×1024 limits).
